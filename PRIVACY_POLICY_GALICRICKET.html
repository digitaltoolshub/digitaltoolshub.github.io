Privacy Policy for Gali Cricket: Tape Ball

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Digital Tools Hub ("we", "us" or "our") provides Gali Cricket: Tape Ball. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including scores, coins, unlocks, settings, records, badges, and streaks. The game does not require an account or sign-in, and this local gameplay information is not uploaded to our servers.

A local analytics log of gameplay checkpoints and events is stored only on your device in offline storage for debugging and performance optimization. It is not transmitted to any developer server or external endpoint.

Advertising and automatically handled information

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads to provide interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. (Banner ads are supported by the SDK but are currently disabled in this build.) In accordance with Google's Play Data Safety disclosure guidelines, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • Approximate location: your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location for ad delivery and fraud prevention;
  • App interactions: interactions such as app launches, taps, and video ad views;
  • Diagnostics: SDK performance and diagnostic metrics, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and
  • Device or other IDs: device and advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and app set ID where applicable.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via your device's Android settings. Advertising behavior and personalization also adhere to your region, consent choices (via Google User Messaging Platform / UMP), and device settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

The game requests standard non-sensitive permissions:

  • Internet access: required to load advertisements and check consent status.
  • Vibration: used solely for in-game tactile haptic feedback during gameplay.

The game does not request or access sensitive device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, storage libraries, or precise GPS location.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed primarily to children under 13. We do not knowingly create accounts for or directly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game does not maintain user accounts or developer-hosted profiles, all local gameplay data remains strictly on your device. You can completely delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's cache and storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained according to Google's published retention policies and your applicable advertising consent configuration.

Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled by the game. Data transmitted by third-party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads) is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date.
