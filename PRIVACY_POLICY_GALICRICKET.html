Privacy Policy for Gali Cricket: Tape Ball

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Digital Tools Hub ("we", "us" or "our") provides Gali Cricket: Tape Ball. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including scores, coins, unlocks, settings, records, badges and streaks. The game does not require an account or sign-in, and this local gameplay information is not uploaded to our servers.

If you choose a custom avatar, the selected or captured image is resized and stored locally on your device. The game does not automatically upload that image to us. If you choose the optional background-removal action, the game opens the third-party remove.bg website in your browser. Information you choose to submit to that service is governed by its own privacy policy.

Advertising and automatically handled information

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads to provide banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. According to Google, its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where applicable.

Google states that this data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention and is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your device settings. Advertising behavior may also depend on your region, consent choices and device settings.

For more information, see Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load advertisements. If you choose to create a custom avatar, the game may ask you to select an image or use a supported camera capture option. These actions are user-initiated. We do not use broad media library access for unrelated purposes.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed primarily to children under 13. We do not knowingly create accounts for or directly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data and custom avatar images remain on your device until you clear the app's storage, use an available reset/remove option, or uninstall the game. We do not maintain a developer-hosted user account or cloud profile for the current version.

Data handled by Google Mobile Ads is retained according to Google's policies and the applicable advertising configuration.

Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled by the game. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date.
