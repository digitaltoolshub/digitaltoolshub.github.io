About Digital Tools Hub

Free, private tools for everyday problems: no sign-ups, no paywalls, no tracking walls.

Digital Tools Hub started with a simple frustration: most “free” online calculators bury the answer under pop-ups, demand an email address, or quietly upload what you type. We think small utilities should be instant, honest and private.

Who builds it

The site is designed and engineered by Rabeeh, a senior developer and automation engineer with a background in data processing, web architecture and security. Each tool is hand-built and tested, rather than assembled from generic plugins, so the maths is transparent and the results are reliable.

How it works

Client-side processing

Tools run entirely in your browser. Passwords, finances and text never touch our servers.

Fast by default

Lightweight pages, no frameworks where they aren’t needed, and offline caching once you’ve visited.

Zero friction

No accounts or paywalls. Open a tool and start working immediately.

Our principles

  • Privacy first. Your inputs stay on your device. Preferences like favourites are kept in your browser’s local storage, which you can clear at any time.
  • Accuracy. Formulas are based on published standards (for example, WHO BMI categories and standard amortization maths), and each tool explains its assumptions.
  • Accessibility. Keyboard navigation, readable contrast and responsive layouts that work on any screen.
  • Sustainability. The site is free thanks to unobtrusive advertising. We never sell personal data.
