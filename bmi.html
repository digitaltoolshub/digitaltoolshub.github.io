About the BMI calculator

How BMI is calculated

Body Mass Index is your weight in kilograms divided by the square of your height in metres: BMI = kg ÷ m². Imperial values are converted first (1 lb = 0.4536 kg, 1 in = 2.54 cm).

What the categories mean

  • Below 18.5: underweight
  • 18.5–24.9: healthy weight
  • 25–29.9: overweight
  • 30 and above: obesity

These are the World Health Organization adult categories. They don’t apply to children, and some populations use lower cut-offs.

Limitations

BMI doesn’t distinguish muscle from fat, so athletes can score “overweight” while being lean. BMI Prime (BMI ÷ 25; aim for 1.0 or below) shows how far you are from the upper healthy limit; a waist measurement is a useful second check. For medical advice, talk to a healthcare professional.
