Estimates are typical mid-market averages for budgeting, not quotes. Exchange rates are converted from USD.

How it works

  • Flights are priced from the great-circle distance between your two cities (cheaper per km on longer routes), scaled by class and season.
  • Hotels are priced per room per night (two travelers share a room) using the destination’s price level.
  • Food, transport and activities are daily per-person costs that scale with the destination’s price level and your travel style.
  • Pick any currency; amounts are calculated in US dollars and converted using live exchange rates when available.

Can’t find your city? Choose Other as the destination and enter the distance and price level yourself.

FAQ

How accurate is the estimate?

It is a planning figure based on typical prices. Real fares and hotel rates vary with booking time, events and availability, so check live prices before you book.

Why does the cost change with destination?

Each destination has its own price level (a day in Zurich costs far more than a day in Hanoi) and its own distance from your home city, which drives the flight price.

Where do exchange rates come from?

Live mid-market rates are fetched when you open the page. If you are offline, built-in approximate rates are used and the note under the currency box says so.
