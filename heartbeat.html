Did You Know?

A blue whale's heart beats only 5-10 times per minute, while a hummingbird's heart can beat up to 1,200 times per minute! The human heart beats about 100,000 times a day, pumping approximately 2,000 gallons of blood.

About the heartbeat calculator

How it works

The calculator multiplies the time since your birth date by an average resting heart rate to estimate how many times your heart has beaten. The live counter keeps adding beats while the page is open.

Typical heart rates

A normal resting heart rate for adults is 60–100 beats per minute. Well-trained athletes are often between 40 and 60. Newborns average 110–160 bpm, which is why lifetime totals are estimates.

Keep your heart healthy

  • Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours and manage stress.
  • See a doctor if your resting rate is regularly above 100 or below 50 without training.
