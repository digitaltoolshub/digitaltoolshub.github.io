Free online tools for everyday tasks

Digital Tools Hub is a growing collection of free online calculators, converters and utilities. Convert currencies with the live currency converter, plan savings with the compound interest calculator, work out discounts with the percentage calculator or check your BMI in seconds.

Developers can format JSON, encode Base64, generate SHA-256 and MD5 hashes and create UUIDs without pasting sensitive data into a server. Designers can compress images, build color palettes and make QR codes.

Every tool is free, works on phones, tablets and desktops, and keeps your data on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up, subscription or usage limit. The site is supported by unobtrusive ads.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Calculations run in your browser. Data you choose to save, such as Kanban tasks or favourites, is stored locally on your device. Live-rate tools fetch public market data but never send your inputs.

Do the tools work offline?

Tools you have opened before are cached and keep working offline. Tools that need live data, such as currency and crypto rates, need a connection to refresh.

Can I install Digital Tools Hub as an app?

Yes. Use the Install app button on the homepage, or choose “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen” in Chrome, Edge or Safari, to use Digital Tools Hub like a native app.

Can I compress images without uploading them?

Yes. The image compressor resizes and re-encodes JPG, PNG and WebP files with your browser's canvas, so photos never leave your device.

How do I suggest a new tool?

Use the contact page to suggest a tool or report a problem. New tools are added regularly.
