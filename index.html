Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up, subscription or usage limit. The site is supported by unobtrusive ads.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Calculations run in your browser. Data you choose to save, such as Kanban tasks or favourites, is stored locally on your device. Live-rate tools fetch public market data but never send your inputs.

Do the tools work offline?

Tools you have opened before are cached and keep working offline. Tools that need live data, such as currency and crypto rates, need a connection to refresh.

Can I install Digital Tools Hub as an app?

Yes. In Chrome, Edge or Safari choose “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen” to use Digital Tools Hub like a native app.
