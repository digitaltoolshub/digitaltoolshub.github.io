About the macro calculator

How your calories are estimated

Your basal metabolic rate (BMR) uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age, then +5 for men or −161 for women. BMR is multiplied by your activity factor to estimate total daily energy expenditure (TDEE), then adjusted for your goal.

How macros are split

Protein and carbohydrates provide 4 kcal per gram and fat provides 9 kcal per gram. Your calorie target is divided between protein, carbs and fat according to the selected goal: higher protein when cutting to preserve muscle, more carbohydrate when bulking.

Tips

  • Re-calculate every 4–6 weeks as your weight changes.
  • Treat the numbers as a starting point and adjust based on real progress.
  • Spread protein across meals (roughly 0.3–0.4 g/kg per meal).
