How to calculate percentages

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. “Per cent” literally means “per hundred”, so 25% is 25/100 or 0.25. Every calculator above is built on that one idea:

  • X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. Useful for tips, commissions and interest.
  • X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. Use it for test scores, budgets and conversion rates.
  • Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ |old| × 100. Positive is an increase, negative a decrease.
  • Increase Y by X% = Y × (1 + X ÷ 100); decrease uses (1 − X ÷ 100).
  • Reverse percentage = part ÷ (X ÷ 100). Find the whole when you know a part and its percentage.

A common trap

A 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease does not bring you back to where you started: 100 → 150 → 75. Each percentage applies to a different base. That's why a stock that drops 50% needs to rise 100% to recover.

FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 80 is 80 × 15 ÷ 100 = 12.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. Going from 50 to 65 is (65 − 50) ÷ 50 × 100 = a 30% increase.

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage points?

Percentage points measure the simple difference between two percentages (5% to 7% is +2 points), while percentage change is relative (5% to 7% is a 40% increase).

How do I find the original price before a discount?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount ÷ 100). A $60 item after a 25% discount originally cost 60 ÷ 0.75 = $80. Use the reverse percentage calculator (number 5) with 75% for this.
