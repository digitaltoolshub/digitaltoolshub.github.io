Privacy Policy for TradeWave

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Educational Simulator Notice: TradeWave is a risk-free cryptocurrency paper-trading and educational platform. The app does not execute real-money transactions, does not connect to real crypto wallets or bank accounts, and does not provide financial or investment advice.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TradeWave ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses your information when you use the TradeWave mobile application on Android. We are committed to transparency, data minimization, and protecting user privacy in compliance with applicable laws and Google Play developer policies.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Collected

Depending on how you use TradeWave (as a guest or signed-in user), we collect the following categories of data:

A. Personal Information (Optional)

  • Account Credentials: If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google, we collect your Email address, Name (display name), and a unique User ID. Guest mode is also available and does not require providing personal credentials.
  • Profile Customization: In-app avatar selection or profile photo URL from your Google account.

B. In-App Activity and Virtual Portfolio Data

  • Paper Trading Portfolio: Virtual cash balance, simulated cryptocurrency holdings, buy/sell trade logs, and performance statistics. For signed-in users, this data is synchronized to Firebase Cloud Firestore so you can access your practice portfolio across devices. For guest users, this data remains stored exclusively on your local device.

C. Financial Information & Purchases

  • In-App Purchases (Subscriptions): If you upgrade to TradeWave Pro, transactions are processed directly by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers or banking information. We and our subscription management provider (RevenueCat) receive transaction confirmation, subscription status, and purchase history tokens to unlock Pro features.

D. Device Identifiers & Analytics (Consented)

  • Firebase Analytics & Google Signals: With your consent via the in-app consent prompt, we collect app interaction events (such as screens viewed, buttons tapped, sign-in methods) and mobile instance identifiers to analyze performance and improve the app. Personal identifiers and trading amounts are never sent to analytics events.
  • Advertising Identifiers (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob): Free-tier users are served advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK accesses the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and device diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in ad delivery. TradeWave requests non-personalized ad delivery within the application. TradeWave Pro subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.

E. AI Chat & RAG Queries (Coach Wavey)

  • When using the Coach Wavey AI assistant with a user-supplied Google Gemini API key, your queries are transmitted to Google's Generative AI API to produce educational responses. The API key is stored securely in your device's hardware keychain using expo-secure-store and is never transmitted to our servers. Offline responses in the Playbook are processed entirely on-device without network transmission.

3. Third-Party SDKs and Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers that process data under strict security standards:

Service Provider Purpose Data Handled
Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics) User authentication, cloud sync for simulated portfolios, and consented usage analytics. Email, Display Name, User ID, Paper trading logs, App interaction events, Device IDs.
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Serving rewarded and interstitial ads to support the free version. Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP-derived approximate location, ad interaction metrics, diagnostics.
RevenueCat Managing Google Play in-app subscriptions and Pro entitlements. App User ID, Purchase history tokens, subscription tier status.
Google Generative AI (Gemini) Optional AI trading coach interactions when configured by the user. User chat prompts sent directly from device via user's API key.
Market Data Providers Fetching real-time cryptocurrency market prices and rendering candlestick charts. Standard HTTP request metadata (IP address). No personal data is sent.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we handle strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and synchronize your practice paper-trading account.
  • Process and verify TradeWave Pro subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • Monitor application stability, fix bugs, and optimize app speed and responsiveness.
  • Display non-personalized ads to users on the free tier.
  • Enforce account security and prevent fraudulent misuse.

5. Data Storage, Encryption, and Security

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app, Firebase, AdMob, RevenueCat, and third-party APIs use industry-standard HTTPS and Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • Local Storage: Guest portfolios, cache, and UI preferences are stored in local device storage. Sensitive keys (such as user-provided Gemini API keys) are stored in the device's hardware keychain via SecureStore.
  • Access Control: Cloud-synced user data is protected by Firebase security rules, restricting data access strictly to authenticated account owners.

6. Account Deletion and Data Retention

We respect your right to control your personal data and provide clear, accessible mechanisms to delete it:

How to Request Account and Data Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your TradeWave account and all associated cloud data at any time:

  • Email Request: Send an email to digitaltoolshub4all@gmail.com with the subject line TradeWave Account and Data Deletion Request. Please send the request from the email address associated with your TradeWave account.
  • What is Deleted: Upon receiving your request, we permanently delete your Firebase Authentication record, Firestore profile document, and all cloud-stored paper trading portfolio records and transaction history. Associated customer identifiers in RevenueCat are also deleted or anonymized.
  • Processing Timeline: Requests are processed within 30 days of verification.
  • Local Data: For guest mode or locally cached data, you can instantly erase all local data by resetting the active account in the app's Profile settings or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

TradeWave is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our servers.

8. Your Choices & Controls

  • Guest Mode: You can practice trading completely anonymously without registering an account.
  • Analytics & Ads Consent: You can review the analytics and advertising consent choices within the app settings.
  • Ad-Free Experience: You can upgrade to TradeWave Pro to remove all ads.
  • API Key Management: You can view, test, or permanently delete your stored Gemini API key from the Coach Wavey settings at any time.
