Privacy policy

Spin Royale Studio · last updated 2 November 2026

What this app is

Spin Royale Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Spin Royale. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos and to run the channel's own live broadcasts. Nobody else signs in to it.

Data it uses

  • The channel owner's YouTube account: to upload and update the channel's videos, playlists and thumbnails, and to create, run and end its live broadcasts.
  • Live chat during the channel's own live shows: the public messages viewers post (display name, channel ID, message text and time), to count predictions (a letter typed in chat), requests for a wheel, and a points leaderboard shown on the stream.
  • Public statistics of the channel's own videos and broadcasts: view, like and concurrent viewer counts.

How it is stored and shared

  • Everything stays on the channel owner's computer: sign-in tokens, logs of the show (with the chat authors' channel IDs, not their messages) and the leaderboard (channel IDs, display names and points).
  • Viewer display names appear on the live stream only if they pass a word filter; they are never spoken. Messages are checked by a local word filter on that computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Show logs and the leaderboard can be deleted at any time by the channel owner; a viewer can ask for their entry to be removed by contacting the channel (below).

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By watching or chatting on the channel's streams you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
