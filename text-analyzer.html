About the text analyzer

What it measures

Word, character, sentence and paragraph counts; average word length; estimated reading and speaking time; a Flesch reading-ease score; and your most frequent keywords with their density.

Readability

Readability formulas such as Flesch Reading Ease use average sentence length and syllables per word. Shorter sentences and simpler words score higher. Most web content should aim for 60 or above.

Privacy

All analysis runs in your browser, so you can safely paste drafts and confidential documents.
